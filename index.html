GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How do I purchase a course?+

Select a course, review your order on its payment page, then pay using a supported UPI app or scan the QR code. Submit your transaction reference for manual verification.

Is my course unlocked immediately?+

No. Submitting a transaction reference does not confirm payment. Your submission stays pending until the payment is independently verified.

Can I learn at my own pace?+

The listed courses are designed as self-paced learning paths. Course access and delivery details should be confirmed with support.
